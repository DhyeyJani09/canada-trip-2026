About the studio

Mosaic is a working archive, kept deliberately plain so the pictures carry the room.

Founded by curator-photographer Renata Vance, the studio shoots on location across Europe, North Africa and East Asia, then edits down hard — most trips yield a single frame worth keeping. Prints are produced in small signed editions on cotton rag; the online wall stays free to browse.
